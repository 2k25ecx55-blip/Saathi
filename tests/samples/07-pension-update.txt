ANNUAL SUBMISSION OF LIFE CERTIFICATE (JEEVAN PRAMAAN) - PENSION CONTINUATION

Respected Pensioner,

As per Central Government Treasury Rules, all central civil pensioners drawing monthly superannuation pension must submit their Annual Life Certificate (Jeevan Pramaan) during the specified window to ensure uninterrupted monthly credit of pension.

PERIOD OF SUBMISSION:
- Senior citizens aged 80 years and above: from 2025-10-01 to 2025-11-30.
- Pensioners below 80 years: from 2025-11-01 to 2025-11-30.
- Absolute final deadline for all pensioners: 2025-11-30.

MODES OF SUBMISSION & WHAT TO CARRY:
You can submit your life certificate using any of the following convenient methods:
1. Digital Life Certificate via Aadhaar Face Authentication app (using your smartphone from home).
2. Through doorstep banking agent / Postman (India Post Payments Bank).
3. In person at your pension disbursing bank branch.

DOCUMENTS NEEDED:
- Pension Payment Order (PPO) number
- Aadhaar Card
- Linked Bank Passbook
- Active mobile phone for receiving OTP

CONSEQUENCES OF NON-SUBMISSION:
If the Life Certificate is not verified by 2025-11-30, pension payment for the month of December 2025 onwards will be automatically stopped by the treasury till subsequent re-verification.
